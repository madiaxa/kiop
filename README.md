# kiop

Kiop is a Tauri-Svelte based music player app teeming with features and optimizations. It should be compatible with all your favorite operating systems (even if you run something like OpenIndiana).

## Features

Kiop is designed to be easy to pick up and learn with a very high ceiling for optimizing your music listening process.

Want to pick 5 songs from your playlist? The keypad can help.

[keypad]

The keypad allows you to pick specific songs on a playlist and play them in a specific order in a flash.

Sound Optimization Settings

Like Audacity, Kiop comes with professional-grade audio mixer settings on a simple music player, even providing the ability to "DJ" your entire playlist on a pre-recorded, song-by-song basis.

## Setup & Installation

The installation process is simple. Go to the releases tag, find your operating system, and download the installer!
